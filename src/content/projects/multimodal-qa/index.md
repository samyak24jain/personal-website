title: "Closed-Domain Multimodal QA"

date: "Jun 01 2024"
---

A multimodal question-answering system built in collaboration with Bosch Research,
focused on technical documents containing graphs, charts, and tables.

I fine-tuned vision-language models — Phi-3-vision, Idefics2, and LLaVA-NeXT —
using LoRA for parameter-efficient training. The resulting system improved
multi-hop QA performance on technical graphs and tables by 12.4%.
